## What can you run on ElfHosted?

I mainly use ElfHosted for my Stremio streaming setup. AIOStreams is the central part of it, bringing together different sources and services such as Comet, MediaFusion and Debridge, with Real-Debrid and TorBox as streaming providers.

I also use JellyStreams and have experimented with several other addons to improve playback reliability, buffering, stream availability and quality.

My setup has evolved over time as I've tested different configurations, compared services and tried to find the best balance between performance, simplicity and cost.

I've been using ElfHosted for a while, although I don't remember the exact date I added each individual app.

**What has your experience with AIOStreams on ElfHosted been so far?**

My experience with AIOStreams on ElfHosted has been mostly positive. It's a powerful tool that lets me combine different streaming sources into one place, making Stremio much more convenient to use.

I've spent quite a bit of time optimizing my setup, especially with Debridge, Real-Debrid, TorBox and different addons. I've occasionally experienced buffering and inconsistent playback performance, which led me to experiment with provider priorities, proxy settings and different configurations.

The biggest downside is that getting everything properly optimized can be confusing, even for someone who's comfortable with technology. However, I really appreciate the flexibility AIOStreams offers, and once configured correctly, it's a very useful part of my streaming setup.

I watch on: Fire TV, Android TV, Smart TV, Tablet.

The apps I watch with: Stremio, Jellyfin.

## What works well?

What works best for me is having different streaming services and addons working together through AIOStreams. I can use Stremio with multiple content sources without having to manage everything separately.

I especially appreciate the flexibility to prioritize providers, customize results and experiment with different configurations. When everything works correctly, the experience is really convenient.

## What goes wrong, and how does it get fixed?

One of the main issues I experienced was occasional buffering and inconsistent playback, even when using premium debrid services.

It wasn't always easy to identify whether the problem came from AIOStreams, a specific addon, the debrid provider or the proxy configuration. I spent time testing different settings and comparing results.

I also contacted ElfHosted's Discord support, where I received helpful advice about using Debridge, avoiding unnecessary proxying with TorBox, and understanding which boosters were actually useful for my setup.

That helped me simplify my configuration and avoid spending money on unnecessary services. I wouldn't say every performance issue has been permanently solved, but the experience has improved, and I appreciated the technical support and willingness to explain how things work.

**How long did the fix take once you asked for help?**

I don't remember the exact response time, surte for not long, but I received helpful advice through ElfHosted's Discord support. Once they explained the correct proxy configuration for Debridge, TorBox and Real-Debrid, I understood what needed to be changed.

The guidance was clear and useful, although testing the different settings and improving playback performance took additional time. It wasn't an instant or permanent fix for every buffering issue.

**Has that problem with AIOStreams happened again since?**

I've experienced occasional buffering again, although the overall streaming performance seems to have improved.

The advice I received from ElfHosted's Discord support was particularly helpful because I learned that some proxy configurations and boosters were unnecessary for my setup, allowing me to save money without sacrificing functionality.

As for the buffering improvements, I believe they may also be related to changing my provider priorities in AIOStreams, placing Real-Debrid ahead of TorBox. However, I can't confirm that this was the actual cause, since streaming performance depends on several factors.

Overall, I'm satisfied with AIOStreams. There is still room for improvement in playback consistency, but the flexibility of the platform and the helpful technical support have made the experience positive.
